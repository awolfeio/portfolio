Scholastic is a leading education company supporting K–12 through digital learning, book fairs, and classroom magazines.

I led the end-to-end redesign of Magazines+, a suite of 20+ companion sites for Grades K–12, used by 250k+ teachers and 13M+ students. This included building a scalable design system to support multiple thematic variations across the platform.

I developed a comprehensive design system with detailed component documentation, usage guidelines, and property specs. All tokens and components were structured in Figma using Variables for export-ready implementation, alongside high-fidelity interactive prototypes for stakeholder alignment.
